## Description

Your notes are files. Your files are yours.

PlainLog is a local-first, plain-text Markdown daily log. No account, no
database, no lock-in — just one Markdown file per day, saved in a folder you
choose on your own device.

**What it is**

PlainLog is intentionally not a bloated notes app. It's built for daily
capture: journaling, task logging, expense logging, and lightweight tagging —
in plain text you can read with any editor, forever.

**How it works**

Pick a folder on your device (or in your own iCloud Drive, if you want
sync). Every day, PlainLog writes one plain .md file — nothing more. There's
no database, no proprietary format, and no hidden files hiding in your
folder. Open the folder in Files, Obsidian, or any text editor, and your
notes are right there.

**Features**

- One Markdown file per day
- Fast Edit and Preview modes
- Simple task, tag, and expense syntax
- A running summary of tasks, tags, and expenses
- Date navigation and a history browser
- Folder Health screen, so you always know your connection status
- Works offline — bring your own sync via iCloud Drive if you want it
- Weekly summary export (Pro)
- Custom themes and font options (Pro)

**Free vs. Pro**

The core daily-capture experience is free — no subscription required for
core features. PlainLog Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks weekly
summary export, custom themes, and font options. No subscription, no
recurring charge, no account required to buy or restore it.

**No lock-in**

If you ever stop using PlainLog, your notes don't stop being yours. They're
already sitting in a folder as plain .md files. Move them, open them
anywhere, keep them forever.

A local-first daily Markdown log for people who hate bloated note apps.

## Keywords

App Store keyword field limit: 100 characters, comma-separated.

PLAN.md §16's full "Primary App Store keywords" list is 10 terms totaling
130 characters of text (139 with separating commas) — over the limit. The
selection below is the highest-value subset that fits:

    local first,offline journal,no subscription,text diary,md notes,plain text journal,markdown journal

Length: 99 characters (limit 100). ✅

**Selection reasoning:** kept the terms most distinct from the App Name /
Subtitle, which already contain "Markdown," "Daily," and "Log" — repeating
those words again in the keyword field has lower marginal search value than
spending the same characters on genuinely different terms. Dropped as a
result:

- `daily log` — "daily" and "log" already covered by the Subtitle/Name.
- `daily markdown` — same reason.
- `plain text log` — "log" already covered; largely redundant with
  `plain text journal`, which is kept.

These three remain valid, PLAN.md-sourced alternates if the selection above
is later revised in App Store Connect (e.g., after real search-term data).
